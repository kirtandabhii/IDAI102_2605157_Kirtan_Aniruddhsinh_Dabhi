            How the numbers work

            Each product type has a multiplier — roughly how many kilograms of CO₂e are emitted per £1 spent on it. Your estimate is price × multiplier, after converting other currencies to pounds. The figures are rounded, awareness-level estimates for comparing choices, not a carbon audit.
